# Paws & Claws Shopify Theme

A custom Shopify eCommerce theme built for **Paws & Claws**, a modern pet supply store focused primarily on dogs and cats.

This theme is designed to feel warm, trustworthy, polished, family-friendly, and professional while still providing a premium shopping experience.

---

## About the Store

**Paws & Claws** is designed as a full-featured pet supply storefront with support for products such as:

- Dog supplies
- Cat supplies
- Toys
- Beds
- Feeding products
- Grooming products
- Collars & leashes
- Travel accessories
- Clothing & accessories
- New arrivals
- Best sellers

---

## Theme Features

### Premium Header & Navigation

- Sticky responsive header
- Desktop mega menus
- Multi-level navigation
- Mobile navigation drawer
- Search
- Customer account access
- Shopping cart
- Live cart count
- Promotional menu areas

### Homepage

The homepage is built as a modular Shopify section system and includes:

- Large hero section
- Shop Dogs / Shop Cats section
- Shop by Category
- Featured Products
- New Arrivals
- Best Sellers
- Moving product showcase
- Dog-focused promotional section
- Cat-focused promotional section
- Why Paws & Claws
- Newsletter signup
- Premium footer

Sections can be reordered, removed, or customized through the Shopify Theme Editor.

### Product Pages

Product pages support:

- Large image gallery
- Product thumbnails
- Product title
- Pricing
- Compare-at pricing
- Sale display
- Variant selectors
- Quantity controls
- Add to Cart
- Buy Now
- Accelerated Shopify checkout
- Product descriptions
- Shipping information
- Returns information
- Product recommendations

### Collection Pages

Collection pages include:

- Responsive product grids
- Shopify-native filtering
- Availability filters
- Price filters
- Sorting
- Product counts
- Sale badges
- Active filter controls
- Mobile filter drawer
- Pagination

### Shopping Cart

The theme includes both a shopping cart page and interactive cart functionality.

Features include:

- Product images
- Product names
- Variant information
- Quantity controls
- Remove buttons
- Subtotal
- Checkout button
- Continue Shopping
- Free-shipping progress indicator
- AJAX cart updates

### Search

The theme includes:

- Store search
- Predictive search functionality
- Search result pages
- Product-focused results

### Additional Pages

Custom page layouts are included for:

- About Us
- Contact Us
- FAQ

The Contact page uses Shopify's native contact form system.

The FAQ page includes expandable accordion sections.
